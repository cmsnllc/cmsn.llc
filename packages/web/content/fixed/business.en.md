Coming Soon LLC works across the following areas, centered on the internet and technology.

## Contract software development

We take on development of web services, business systems and applications, covering design, implementation and operation to fit each client's needs.

## Web planning and development

We plan, build and run websites for companies, schools and individuals, from the first idea through operation after launch.

## Nok Audio

We run [Nok Audio](https://nok.audio), a brand that develops Eurorack synthesizer modules. We also perform live and produce music.

## Education

We hold programming workshops at local schools, including junior high schools.

## Environment and community

As a Kawasaki SDGs Gold Partner and a member of the [30by30 Alliance](/en/p/1), we support efforts toward biodiversity conservation and a sustainable community.
